# LegalMindAI — Known Limitations & Operational Boundaries

This document articulates the legal, technical, and architectural boundaries of LegalMindAI.

---

## 1. Statutory & Corpus Scope Boundaries

1. **Central Acts vs. Subordinate State Rules**:
   The primary indexed corpus covers Central Legislation (India Code), the Constitution of India, and landmark Supreme Court decisions. Subordinate State Rules, municipal bylaws, and specialized local administrative notifications are not universally pre-indexed. Users analyzing state-specific disputes should upload relevant local enactments via `POST /documents/upload` or the CLI `/upload` command.
2. **Subordinate Court Records**:
   District Court, Sessions Court, and Magistrate Court records are not pre-indexed due to jurisdictional dispersion. LegalMindAI supports trial court workflows through user document upload (FIRs, charge-sheets, bail petitions).

---

## 2. Temporal Law & Real-Time Gazette Notifications

1. **Criminal Law Replacement Boundary (1 July 2024)**:
   The system models the transition from IPC, CrPC, and IEA to BNS, BNSS, and BSA. However, state-specific amendments enacted post-July 2024 must be verified against respective State Official Gazettes.
2. **Pending Constitutional Challenges**:
   Judicial challenges pending before larger Constitution Benches are noted as sub-judice where documented in the precedent graph; final outcomes depend on ongoing court determinations.

---

## 3. Multimodal & OCR Boundaries

1. **Degraded Historical Documents**:
   While `ocr_service.py` provides non-destructive confusion detection for degraded print, severely corrupted carbon-copy police diaries or low-resolution handwritten affidavits may require manual transcript verification.
2. **Audio Voice Typing**:
   Voice typing operates via local Whisper models. In environments with high ambient background noise, verification of legal statutory numbers before submitting is recommended.

---

## 4. Legal Regulatory & Liability Boundaries

1. **Not Formal Legal Advice**:
   Under the **Advocates Act, 1961** and the **Bar Council of India Rules**, LegalMindAI does not engage in the practice of law, provide legal counsel, or establish an advocate-client relationship.
2. **Professional Verification Required**:
   All citations, statutory section numbers, limitation periods, and procedural timelines generated by the assistant must be independently reviewed and verified by a licensed advocate before filing in any judicial forum.
